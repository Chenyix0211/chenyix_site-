About CHENYIX PUBLICATIONS

CHENYIX PUBLICATIONS is a dedicated academic publishing house committed to crafting high-quality, examination-focused textbooks that empower students across India.

With our motto "Blueprint Your Future", we bring together expert educators and a meticulous editorial process to deliver books that are clear, comprehensive, and aligned with current syllabi — including ICSE, CBSE and State Boards.

Founded with a vision to make quality education accessible, CHENYIX PUBLICATIONS bridges the gap between academic curriculum and student understanding through thoughtfully designed, examination-ready content.

New Initiative

Faculty Development Programme (FDP)

Empowering STEM Educators Across India

CHENYIX PUBLICATIONS proudly introduces the Faculty Development Programme (FDP) — a structured initiative designed exclusively for faculties from STEM disciplines (Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics).

Our FDPs bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world pedagogical excellence. Whether you are a school teacher, college lecturer, or institutional academic coordinator, our programme offers curated sessions that elevate teaching practice, align curricula with current board requirements, and integrate environmental and scientific awareness into daily classroom delivery.

Updated STEM pedagogical techniques
Environmental science curriculum integration
ICSE / CBSE syllabus alignment workshops
Support for NAAC & accreditation preparation
Collaborative sessions with expert authors & editors
